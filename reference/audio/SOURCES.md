archaeology is needed later. A row without a final asset is `PENDING USER ASSET`
until the real WAV arrives; nothing else in the app depends on this file.

## Status

The five premium WAVs currently packaged (`res/raw/blip_glass.wav`,
`blip_wood.wav`, `blip_soft_bell.wav`, `blip_bonk.wav`, `blip_space.wav`) are
**synthesized placeholders** generated by `scripts/make_placeholder_sounds.py`.
They are not release assets and must be replaced with the user's curated final
set before store release. Stable ids `sound_glass` / `sound_wood` /
`sound_soft_bell` / `sound_bonk` / `sound_space` are already live in settings
persistence, trials and Billing products — when the final WAVs arrive they
replace the asset files (and optionally the display names); the ids do not move.

When a final asset lands, record it here and run the audio QA list from the
T-28 brief (valid WAV, SoundPool decode, length, clipping, silence, loudness vs
Original, encoding, size, dead air) before deleting the placeholder script.
